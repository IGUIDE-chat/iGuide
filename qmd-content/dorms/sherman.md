# Sherman Hall

## Overview

Sherman Hall is a conveniently located residence hall near the Main Quad, popular with graduate students and upper-division undergraduates. It is close to academic buildings, recreation centers, shops, and restaurants, and remains open during Fall, Winter, and Spring Breaks.
