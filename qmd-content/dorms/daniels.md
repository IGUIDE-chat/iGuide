# Daniels Hall

## Overview

Daniels Hall is a Green Street graduate and upper-division hall that stays open during university breaks and offers both academic-year and 12-month contracts, with individual-use and semi-private bathroom layouts.

## Location

Area: Campustown
Address: 1010 W. Green St.
Coordinates: 40.1108189773823, -88.2218052908465

- Bathroom: semi-private
- Housing Type: URH
